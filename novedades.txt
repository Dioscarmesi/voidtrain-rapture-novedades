[en]
<b><color=#FFB85A>ALPHA 0.1 · November 12, 2026</color></b>

• <b>New name:</b> the world is now called VoidTrain Rapture.
• <b>Tables on tiles:</b> up to 12 workbenches on your wagon, placed anywhere from BUILD.
• <b>Survival:</b> hunger drops over time. Eat from your backpack by tapping a food.
• <b>The predator stalks:</b> it circles far away, stops to stare and charges. Shoot it while it holds still.
• <b>Fishing:</b> aim the hook at fish schools to get food.
• <b>Stacked backpack:</b> 24 different items, 64 of each.
• <b>Daily reward:</b> log in every day for materials (7-day cycle).

Thanks for trying the alpha. Updates every week!
